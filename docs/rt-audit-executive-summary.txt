================================================================================
ROOT CAUSE
================================================================================

The RT engine (engines/rt/compile.py) is sophisticated—it parses addresses,
geocodes them, extracts company metadata, and identifies law firms. However,
the Compiler (cleo/compiler/writer.py Pass 3) applies a "schema-first" approach:

  "Write only what fits the schema. Everything else → JSON."

This worked in the early stages but creates data loss as the pipeline matured.
The data is RIGHT THERE in clean-data/rt/*.json files, fully normalized and
geocoded, but never reaches the database.
