# Atom Exploration Report — 2026-04-22

Read-only analysis of `party_fingerprints` + `party_atoms`.

## Headline Takeaways

- **Scale**: 249,084 party-sides carrying 1,073,909 atoms across 99,984 distinct brand_token values — the raw material for portfolio discovery.
- **Address coverage dominates; contact coverage is thin**: postal codes on 81% of party-sides, phones on 39%, contact_fingerprints on 72%. Address-based atoms will be the primary clustering signal.
- **Brand token IDF skew is severe**: the top tokens are generic corporate-vocabulary ('ontario', 'individual', 'named', 'holdings', 'canada') that appear on tens of thousands of party-sides. Approximately 61% of distinct brand_tokens are singletons — they appear exactly once and have zero clustering value. Effective discovery requires IDF weighting and a generic-token exclusion list.
- **'Named Individual(s)' is the largest single party cluster** with ~43,500 party-sides — 17% of all party-sides. These are suppressed-name records and will need special handling (they share no brand identity, only address/phone atoms when present).
- **Kingsett pivot validates the system**: the brand_token='kingsett' anchor returns 367 matching party-sides with 66 Wellington Street as the top address and consistent phone/contact co-signals — confirming that atom-based co-occurrence works as expected for known large portfolios.
